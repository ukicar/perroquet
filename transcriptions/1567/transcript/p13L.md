notes:
- 01 "xxiij.e" (23 October 1567); between octobre and v.c a small sign like a 9 or m with a stroke, probably m(il); "v.c" = cinq cens.
- 08, 13 "Dampville": written Dampuille (Damville, i.e. Henri de Montmorency-Damville).
- 09 "majesté": written in full with an accent, maiesté.
- 14 "arbitrer": a-r-b-i-t-r-e-r (a first reading advertir was wrong).
- 16 "necess.[aires?]": necess + a superscript that reads aires or res.
- 17 "seurté": so written (s-e-u-r-t-é).
- 19 "[estans?]": estans or estant.
- 27 "La Cour" in large display letters.
