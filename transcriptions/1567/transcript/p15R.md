notes:
- 01 "prethendue": so written, with th (pretendue on fol. 4r).
- 05 "[Baudoyn?]": b-a-u-d-o-y-n with a small initial.
- 08 "Thl.se": T-h-l + superscript se (Tholose).
- 17 "iiij.e" (4): 4 December 1567 was a Thursday (Julian), which agrees with Jeudy.
- 19 "[Malran?]": the name ends with two minims here (Malrac on fol. 2r); "[Benoist?]": cramped (a second reader read Bauvet).
- 21 "de gaugan" with a lower-case g (the other occurrences have the capital glyph); "[Ouvoir?]": as on fol. 2r.
- 22 "Sur" in large display letters.
