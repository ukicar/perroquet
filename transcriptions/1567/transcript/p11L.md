notes:
- 01 "sur ce" written in one run; "[que?]": que or qui.
- 02 "a [arrio?] ses Comptes de Cour": a short word a-r-r-i-o (arrester? ouir?) ; "de Cour" is followed by a comma.
- 06 "[ventes/biens?]": the word begins with the scribe's b/v glyph; both readings fit.
- 09 "qui seroit": no l after qui.
- 10 the day reads xix.e (19); 19 November 1567 was a Wednesday (Julian), which agrees with mercredy.
- 11 "Mess.rs": M + e + ss + superscript rs.
- 12 "[Malrac?]": the final letter is unclear (Malras read by a second reader); "Rangouse": R-a-n-g-o-u-s-e; "[Roguier?]": Roquier not excluded.
- 13 "doussat": the middle is a long double s (Doujat on fol. 2r); "Bouald." is followed by a capital P and a dash.
- 14 the last two names as on fol. 2r line 06.
- 25 "[Thl.?]": the city name is abbreviated T + h/o + l with a stroke (cf. Thl.se on fol. 11r line 08).
