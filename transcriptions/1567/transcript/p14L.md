notes:
- 06 "[Seig.r?]": S + e/r + g with superscript r (seigneur); the same sign on fol. 10r line 01.
- 07 "[se?]": se or si ("et si pour les necessitez ... Il y auroit aucuns deniers").
- 08 "[p(rese)nteront?]": p with a stroke + minims; "[puis?]": p-u-i-s (the sense wants "si").
- 15 "[du. Si?] nommé commis et depputé": the first two words are cramped (dud. S.r ? Et si ?); "commis" is written with a contraction.
- 24 "\par/" is added above the line between "que" and "les".
