notes:
- 01 "Remonstre": a flourish precedes the capital R (a second reader read "A remonstre").
- 05 "[p(rese)ntée?]": written p-n/u-t-é-e; read as the scribe's abbreviation of presentée (cf. fol. 5r "p(rese)ntz", "p(rese)nce"), but the stroke on the p is not clearly visible; a second reader read portée.
- 06 "[remué?]": r-e-m-u-é (remis not excluded); cf. line 10 remuement.
- 08 "et f(air)e": the et-sign followed by f with a superscript stroke (remonstrer et faire requisition).
- 26 "[prethendue?]": an h-like ascender after the t, as on fol. 11r line 01 (pretendue on line 21 of this page).
- 07 "[Daygua?]": D-a-y-g-u-a, the advocate general's name, as on fol. 3v (where the first letter looked like De-).
- 10 "[remuement?]": r-e-m-u-e-m-e-n-t with cramped minims; "relaxement" not excluded.
- 11 "a [f(air)e?]": a superscript abbreviation f + stroke.
- 21 "[nouvelle?]": n-o-u-u-e-l-l-e, the ll cramped (nommée not excluded); a short mark stands above the word between the lines.
- 27 "<Ilz>": a word struck through before "la".
