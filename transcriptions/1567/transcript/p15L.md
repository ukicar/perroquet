notes:
- 01 "[que?] f(air)e ce pourra": the formula is "que faire ce pourra"; the first word looks like qui, and faire is the abbreviated f with stroke.
- 02 "f(air)e": f with a superscript stroke.
- 05 "deuem.t": deuem + superscript t (deuement); a first reading d'heure was wrong.
- 09 "xxvij.e" (27): this assembly of 27 November is entered after the Friday 28 heading.
- 10 "assemblez": the ending is the scribe's -ez flourish (assemblée not excluded).
- 23 "En lad. assemblée": the first word could be read Et.
- 24 "commettre": committre not excluded.
- 13 "[Malrac?]": a second reader read Malras; "[Latoury?]": as on fol. 8r (Latomy intended?); "Nicolas" written Nicolas/Nicolae.
- 14 "Cons.er": Con + superscript er.
- 19 "[traitter?]": traitter or traicter.
- 22 "[obeyssance?]": the second vowel is unclear (oboyssance as written?).
