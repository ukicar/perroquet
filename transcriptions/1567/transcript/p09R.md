notes:
- 06, 20, 26 "p(rese)ntz", "p(rese)nce", "p(rese)ns": p with a stroke through the descender + ntz / nce / ns.
- 13 "f(air)e": f with a superscript stroke; "[et/de?] tesmoins": the sign before tesmoins resembles the scribe's et-sign; a second reader took it for "de".
- 22 "Ce reg.re": ce registre (the registre ordered on line 17); a first reading "Icelle requeste" was wrong.
- 23 "sur ce" written in one run (surce).
- 25 "Dud. Jour": D-u-d with the large flourished initial (dudit jour); "Aud." not excluded. The numeral reads x + b-shaped v + iij with superscript e = xviij.e (18), the same day as fol. 3v line 23.
