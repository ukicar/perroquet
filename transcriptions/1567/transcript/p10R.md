notes:
- 07, 08 see fol. 5v note on the treasurer's name.
- 10, 26 "Con.el": Con + superscript el (conseil).
- 11 "M.rs Sieurs": M with superscript rs, then Sieurs written in full.
- 12, 13 "[Dausour?]": D, a small mark, then a-u-ſ-o-u-r, the same both times (Daussour, Dansour possible; a second reader read Daussoux).
- 20 "[l'annee?]": a capital L/J, then a-n-u/n-i-e and a flourish: l'annee or Janvier (en Janvier mil v.c Lxij).
- 22 "de Strocy": written with the scribe's de-sign (ꝺ) before Strocy; Evesque is written Euesque.
- 26 "[qui?] parle": the formula is "qui parle" (lines 14, 21) but the word looks like que here.
- 27 "[tancé?]": the initial is a looped capital (T or C), then a-n-c-é; tancé (reprimanded) fits the sense.
