notes:
- 02 "[dit?] lors": d-i/u-t; "du" would give no sense.
- 03, 22 "surintendance": written sur + ſutridance in one run (superintendance not excluded).
- 12 "f(air)e": f with a superscript stroke (se mesloient de faire lesd. ventes); "aut(res)": au + stroke.
- 21 "Et La Cour" in large display letters.
