notes:
- 07, 24 the advocate general's name: D-e-y-g-u-a (Deygua / Daygua); line 24 is in large display letters and reads the same.
- 08 "[Cunyes?]": the treasurer general's name, C-u-u/n-n-y-e-s; the minims are ambiguous (Cunnyes, Cuunyes); the same name on fol. 5v, 6r, 9r, 9v, 10r is read [Cunyez?].
- 09 "g(e)n(er)al": written g(e)n(er)al with a contraction stroke; same on 24.
- 15 "[gendarmerie?]": written in one run, g-e-n-d-a-r-m-e-r-i-e with cramped minims.
- 13 "[Tesse?]": see fol. 3r line 24 (Tisse possible).
- 18 "et la charge": the sign before "la charge" is the scribe's et-sign (as in "et deffence" on the line above), although "A la charge" is the expected formula.
- 23 the day reads xviij.e (18) with the v in the scribe's b-like form; a second reader counted xviiij possible.
