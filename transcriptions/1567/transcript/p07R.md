notes:
- 04 "[Pour?]": a word with a large flourished initial between the Et-sign and pourvoir; Pour (Et pour pourvoir) fits, Donc not excluded. "pourvoir" is written here without the e (pourvoir on fol. 6v).
- 05 "leurd.": no s visible. "[cons.el?]": cons + a superscript flourish; 06 "commun de la ville" follows (conseil commun).
- 07, 19 "la [Caunye?]": C-a-u-n/u-y-e; the same spelling both times.
- 14 "commutation": the minims could also be read communtation.
- 16 "[aucunes?]": a short abbreviated word au- + s; aultres also possible.
- 24 "Andri": A-n-d-r-i with the dot on the i. "[Tesse?]": T + a vowel (e or i) + sse; a second reader read Tisse; the same merchant on fol. 3v line 13.
- 26 "[despance?]": one letter overwritten by the scribe.
