# Transcription conventions — BnF ms. Français 5455, 1567 section

Source: Gallica scan `Extraits_des_registres_du_parlement_[...]_btv1b9065178t.pdf`
(Parlement de Toulouse, "Extrait particulier de tout ce qui s'est faict au Parlement
pendant la Ligue et années 1567 finissant 1569").

Scope of this transcription: the entries of 1567 = PDF page 5 (right side) and PDF
pages 7–17 (page 17 left side only) = folios 2r–12v as numbered in the manuscript
(13 November – 11 December 1567). PDF page 6 is a BnF placeholder card ("Reliure
serrée"), not a text page.

## Files

- `images/pNNS_full.png` — the whole text block of one folio page. NN = PDF page
  number, S = L (left page of the spread = verso) or R (right page = recto).
- `images/pNNS_1.png`, `_2.png`, `_3.png` — three overlapping strips of that page, top
  to bottom. They overlap by about 130 px, so the last line of one strip reappears
  at the top of the next one: transcribe it once.
- `transcript/pNNS.md` — first-pass transcription (Claude, pass 1).
- `verify/pNNS.md` — independent second reading (separate agents, pass 2).
- `1567_transcription.md` — reconciled text, all pages.

## Rules

1. One transcript line per manuscript line, numbered `01`, `02`, … from the top of
   the page: `07  text of line seven`. Leave one blank line between acts (where the
   manuscript starts a new dated heading or a new paragraph with a large initial).
2. Spelling exactly as written: no modernisation, no normalisation of doubled
   consonants, no added letters. Accents: only the acute on a final tonic
   -é / -és / -ée(s) where the sense requires it (ordonné, levée); no other accents.
3. i/j and u/v are rendered by their modern value (avoir, une, Jeudy).
   Word separation and apostrophes follow modern usage (d'icelle, s'est, de tout).
4. Abbreviations are NOT expanded. Keep the abbreviated form, with a period at the
   suspension and any superscript letters written after the period:
   `lesd.` `aud.` `desd.` `lad.` `Mess.rs` `Comm.res` `cons.er` `Sr.` `M.e`
   `v.c` (= cinq cens, the "v" with superscript "c") `sep.bre` `reg.res` `Tholoze`.
   Contractions marked by a stroke or tilde over the word (not suspensions) get the
   supplied letters in round brackets: `declara(ti)on`, `p(ar)`.
   Note: in this hand the letter v often looks like a b or a 6 (`v.c lxvij`); the same
   б-shaped glyph serves as capital G, S and B (Gaugan, Sabatier, Bouald), so initials of
   names are read from the recurring lists, not from the glyph alone. The scribe writes
   `pourvoir` (pouruoir), `neaumoins`, `rebillion`, `bestail`, `naguieres`.
5. The ampersand-like sign for "et" is written `et`.
6. The dashes the scribe uses to fill line ends are omitted. The oblique stroke `/`
   that closes an act is kept. No punctuation is added; the manuscript's commas and
   strokes are kept.
7. Capitals as in the manuscript when clearly distinguishable, otherwise modern usage.
8. Doubt and editorial marks (square brackets are reserved for doubt):
   - `[word?]`   — a reading you are not sure of
   - `[a/b?]`    — two possible readings
   - `[...]`     — illegible
   - `<text>`    — text struck through in the manuscript
   - `\text/`    — text added above the line
   - `[sic]`, `[note: ...]` — editorial remarks
9. Roman numerals as written (`xiij.e`, `v.c lxvij`), never converted.
10. Marginal notes are given at the end of the page as `margin: ...`. The folio
    number written at the top right of rectos is given in the page header, not as a line.

## Page header format

```
# p07R — fol. 3r (folio number as written: 3)
```
