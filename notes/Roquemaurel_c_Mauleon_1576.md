# Roquemaurel c. Mauléon — Parlement de Toulouse, 1575-1576

Search made on 6 October 2026 in the digitised registers of the Archives départementales de la
Haute-Garonne (AD31), série 1 B, "Arrêts civils de la Grand Chambre et de la chambre des Enquêtes".
Images fetched from the AD31 IIIF server. Conventions as in `transcription_1567/CONVENTIONS.md`
(abbreviations kept, `[word?]` = uncertain reading, `[...]` = illegible). This is a first-pass reading by
Claude; it has not been checked by a second reader.

## 1. Register 1 B 73 is not the 1575 register

1 B 73 covers November 1575 – April 1576. The register for 25 June 1575 is **1 B 72**
(30 April – 10 November 1575, 586 images). AD31 call numbers match the 1903 printed inventory
(the inventory's "B 62", Nov. 1567 – Nov. 1568, is the downloaded file 1B62).

## 2. 1 B 72, sitting of Saturday 25 June 1575 — no entry for the decree

- Folio 178v (image 179, left page): "Vendredy vingt quatriesme Juing mil v.c lxxv" (feast of
  St John the Baptist, no sitting), then "Samedy vingtcinquiesme dud. mois de Juing en la grand chambre".
- The sitting runs to folio 181v. Folio 182r opens "Lundy vingtseptiesme de Juing mil v.c lxxv".
- Entries of 25 June, by rapporteur in the margin:
  - Grand Chambre: de Prohenques (a Fontan, marchant of Limoux); Buet (two entries); a third rapporteur
    (Jacques Mage, marchant of Limoux); Richard (petition of the clergy of the dioceses of Comminges and
    Couserans, f. 179v, pronounced 1 July); Buet (clergy of the diocese of Lombez, f. 180r); petition of
    Rigal Ouvrier, conseiller (f. 180v).
  - Chambre criminelle: de Montfort (procureur général against a canon of Lavaur).
  - Première chambre des Enquêtes: Sabatery; de Magnac (two entries, one of a Guilhot of Colomiers).
  - Seconde chambre des Enquêtes: Cammelz (petition of a community syndic).
- None concerns Roquemaurel, Soueix, Soulan or Mauléon. The decree of adjudication was not copied into
  the arrêts register. Decrees (sales by decree of seized lands) were registered separately.

## 3. 1 B 74, folio 198r (image 199, right page) — order to execute the decree, July 1576

Margin, later hand (the 19th-c. inventory compiler), boxed: `de Roquemaurel c. de Mauléon`.
Left margin: the rapporteur's name, `[Caliste?]`, and a fee mark `iij [?]`.

```
00  Prononcé le vj[?] Juillet

01  Entre Guillaume de Roquemaurel s.r de Suys demandeur
02  en execution de decret d'une part et Jehan Anthoine
03  de Mauleon s.r du lieu de [Mauleon?] opposant d'aultre
04  Veu les plaidez et app.t du xj [Janvier?] dernier, le
05  decret obtenu de la Court par led. Roquemaurel du xxv
06  de Juing mil v.c lxxv, [...] de proces verbal sur
07  l'execution d'icelluy, requeste presentée par led. demandeur
08  ensemble [...] [presentée?] contre l'opposant
09  affin de forclusion de produire et exploictz sur ce faictz
10  Il sera dict que la Court sans avoir esgard a l'oppo(siti)on
11  dud. Mauleon de laquelle l'a demis et desmect a ordonné
12  et ordonne que led. decret sera executé en ce qui reste
13  entre les opposans ainsi qu'il appartiendra et condamne
14  led. opposant es despens de lad. oppo(siti)on la taxe
15  reservée /
```

Notes:
- 01 "Suys" = Soueix, as in the 1555 act cited by the family history ("habitant de Suys").
- 03 the place name after "s.r du lieu de" is unclear; Mauleon fits the letters but is not certain.
- 04 the month after "xj" is cramped; Janvier fits (11 January 1576, "dernier" = of this year).
- 05-06 **the decree is dated 25 June 1575**, which confirms the date given by the family history.
- 06 one or two words before "de proces verbal" are illegible at this resolution.
- 12 "en ce qui reste" implies the decree had been partly executed already.
- The "Prononcé" line sits above the entry. The family history gives 6 July 1576 for this order,
  which agrees with the reading vj.

## Where the decree itself should be

Not in the arrêts registers. Look for the Parlement's registers of decrees and adjudications
(saisies réelles) for 1575, or the newly re-established Chambre des Requêtes (re-established June 1575,
1 B 72 f. 147). The family history cites it as "A.M.T tome II, p. 502", a reference not yet identified.
